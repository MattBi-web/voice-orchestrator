# TV & Decoder Troubleshooting (Meridian Telecom)

## Step 1 — Check the decoder standby light
A solid light means the decoder has power and network; a blinking light means it's still booting
(wait 2 minutes) or has lost connection to the network (restart it).

## Step 2 — Missing channels
Channels missing after a plan upgrade are refreshed by unplugging the decoder for 15 seconds —
the channel list doesn't update live, it needs a reboot to re-pull the new plan's lineup.

## Step 3 — Remote not responding
Check batteries first; if replacing them doesn't help, re-pair the remote by holding the decoder's
front button for 5 seconds until the LED flashes twice, then point the remote and press any key.

## Step 4 — Picture freezing/pixelating
This is almost always a connectivity issue between the decoder and the router, not the TV service
itself — check the decoder's network connection (wifi signal or ethernet cable) before escalating
as a service fault.
