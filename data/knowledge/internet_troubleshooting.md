# Internet Troubleshooting (Meridian Telecom)

## Step 1 — Check the router lights
A solid green internet light means the connection to Meridian's network is fine — the problem is
likely wifi/device-side. A red or blinking internet light means the issue is on the line itself.

## Step 2 — Power cycle
Unplug the router for 30 seconds, plug it back in, wait 2 minutes for full reboot. This resolves
roughly 60% of "no connection" calls — always do this before escalating.

## Step 3 — Wifi vs wired
If wired devices work but wifi doesn't, it's a wifi-band/interference issue, not a line fault —
suggest switching the device to the 5GHz band or moving closer to the router.

## Step 4 — Line fault
If the internet light is red/blinking after a power cycle, this is a line fault requiring a
technician visit — do not keep troubleshooting at this point, schedule a visit.

## Known regional issue
There is a known fiber maintenance window affecting the Milano-Est exchange, Tuesdays 2-4 AM —
brief drops during that window are expected and don't need a ticket.
