# Billing Policy (Meridian Telecom)

## Payment due dates
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and due within 20 days. A reminder SMS is sent 5 days
before the due date.

## Late payment
A payment overdue by more than 10 days incurs a €5 late fee. Service is suspended (not terminated)
after 30 days overdue; reactivation is free once the balance is paid.

## Refunds
Refunds for billing errors are processed within 5 business days to the original payment method. A
refund for a cancelled add-on is prorated to the day of cancellation, never a full-month credit.

## Disputing a charge
Ask the caller for the specific charge and date before escalating — most disputes are resolved by
walking through the itemized charges on the account lookup, not by offering a credit outright.
