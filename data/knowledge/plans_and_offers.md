# Plans & Offers (Meridian Telecom)

## Current plans
- **Base** — 100 Mbps fiber, no TV: €24.90/month.
- **Plus** — 500 Mbps fiber + 50 TV channels: €34.90/month.
- **Max** — 1 Gbps fiber + 120 TV channels + streaming bundle: €44.90/month.

## Upgrade policy
Upgrading mid-cycle is prorated for the current month and takes effect within 24 hours (fiber
plans) or immediately (TV channel changes). No upgrade fee.

## Current promotion
New Plus or Max activations get the first 3 months at 50% off. Existing customers upgrading from
Base qualify for 1 month at 50% off (not 3) — don't conflate the two offers.

## Contract terms
All plans are month-to-month, no minimum commitment, 30-day cancellation notice.
